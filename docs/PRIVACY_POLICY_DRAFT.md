# Privacy Policy Draft — ABC Kids

**Draft only. This is not legal advice and must be reviewed by qualified counsel and completed with the publisher's legal identity, contact details, jurisdictions and final service list before release.**

ABC Kids is designed for children and families. Children can use learning activities without creating an account. In this MVP, child activity and optional avatar/settings are stored on the device. The app does not request precise location, contacts, camera, microphone recordings, chat, or behavioral advertising identifiers. The app has no ads or analytics SDK enabled.

The device's built-in speech engine may be used to speak letters and words. Speech text is handled by the operating system's available voice service; ABC Kids does not record or upload a child's voice.

A parent may optionally use email-code authentication if the publisher configures Supabase. The parent's email and authentication session are used only for parent access. This MVP does not upload child progress for cloud backup. Purchases are currently simulated only in development builds; a production release will identify its store provider and explain purchase and transaction data handling here.

Parents may contact **[publisher contact required]** with privacy questions or requests. Before publication, add verified instructions for parent requests, deletion/retention, subprocessors, international transfers, legal bases, regional rights and effective date.
