## Future upload checklist

1. Confirm YouTube Studio is switched to **ABC Friends** / `@ABCFriendsApp` before selecting a file. The account switcher can make the personal channel look similar.
2. Use the reviewed, final export from `assets/play-store/` and confirm it is the intended cut, landscape framing, and has the approved voice-over, music, and readable captions. Do not regenerate or replace a video without the required creative and credit review in the promo-video skill.
3. Set a clear title and an accurate description based on features verified in the current app. Keep the current promo's title and description as a reference, not as fixed text for unrelated videos.
4. Set the audience accurately. For videos directed to young children, select **Made for Kids**. YouTube may disable comments, notifications, and personalized ads for this audience setting.
5. Review the thumbnail and preview, wait for upload and processing, and check copyright results before choosing visibility.
6. Set **Public** only when publishing is requested. Check the channel name, title, audience, and visibility on the final screen before publishing, then verify YouTube's published confirmation and save the resulting video link here when useful.

## Authorization notes

- Uploading a file and publishing it are separate actions. Follow the user's instruction for the specific video and destination channel; do not infer approval to publish future videos from this one upload.
- If YouTube presents a Terms of Service or similar legally binding acknowledgment, obtain the user's confirmation at that time before accepting it.
- Do not share the video to social networks or send messages as part of upload unless the user separately asks.
