# ABC Friends Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2, 2026**

ABC Friends is an offline-first learning app for children and caregivers. A child can use the learning activities without creating an account. A child’s optional nickname, avatar, settings, and learning progress stay on the device. On Android, a non-personalized ad may appear only in the parent-gated dashboard while Premium is inactive. An adult may buy or restore a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play.

## 1. Who this policy is for

ABC Friends is made for young children and their parents or caregivers. This policy explains what information stays on the device, what may be shared with service providers, and how parents can manage or delete it.

**App operator:** ABC Friends  
**Privacy contact:** [info@021labs.online](mailto:info@021labs.online)

## 2. Information kept on the device

A parent may choose a short nickname and an animal avatar for a child. ABC Friends also saves the selected app language and sound settings, letters viewed or traced, activities played, stars, and stickers. This information stays on the device. ABC Friends does not send the child’s nickname, avatar, or learning progress to us.

Please do not use a nickname that includes a child’s full name, school, address, phone number, or other private details. The operating system or a device backup service may copy app data according to the family’s device settings; that backup is controlled by the device provider, not ABC Friends.

## 3. Speech and sound

When voice is turned on, ABC Friends asks the device’s built-in text-to-speech service to speak letters and words. The app does not record a child’s voice or use speech recognition. The device or speech-service provider may handle the text under its own settings and privacy terms. Bundled animal sounds play from the device and are not uploaded by ABC Friends.

## 4. What we do not use

ABC Friends does not run its own behavioral advertising or user analytics, social features, public child profiles, or child-to-child messaging. It does not ask for a child’s precise location, contacts, photos, camera access, or microphone recordings. We do not sell children’s information.

## 5. Sharing and service providers

We do not share a child’s profile or learning progress. On Android, Google Mobile Ads and its consent tools may automatically collect and share approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions such as launches and taps, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Ads are child-directed, non-personalized, and limited to the parent area. The Android advertising-ID permission is blocked, but other identifiers may still be processed. Google Play and RevenueCat process adult purchase history and purchase status, including any legacy subscription still in its paid period; they do not receive the child’s nickname, profile, or learning progress. If a parent emails us, we receive the email address and message so we can reply. We may disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect a person’s safety, the app, or our legal rights.

Apple, Google, and device operating-system providers may separately process information when families download or use their services. Their own privacy policies apply.

## 6. Purchases and ads

An adult can make a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play. Before purchase, Google Play shows the localized price and charges the Google Play account once; there is no automatic renewal. Existing subscribers may retain Premium through the end of a period they have already paid for; Google Play manages any such legacy subscription renewal or cancellation. Google Play handles payment details; RevenueCat verifies purchase status to unlock Premium and restore access. ABC Friends does not collect card details. A non-personalized Google Mobile Ads banner may appear only in the parent-gated dashboard on Android while Premium is inactive; it does not appear in child activities and is not rewarded. Google Play, RevenueCat and Google Mobile Ads process their respective information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## 7. How long information is kept and deletion

Information stored on the device remains there until a parent resets the relevant app data, clears ABC Friends from the device, or uninstalls the app. **Reset all progress** clears learning activity, stars, and stickers; the child nickname, avatar, and preferences remain. To remove all app data, use the device’s app-storage controls or uninstall ABC Friends. A device backup may retain a copy according to device-provider settings.

We keep support emails only as long as needed to answer and follow up on the request or meet legal and security duties.

## 8. Parents’ choices and rights

Parents can turn off voice in app settings and reset the child’s local learning progress at any time. Rights available under local privacy law may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or a complaint to a privacy regulator.

## 9. Security

We limit the information the app needs and use reasonable safeguards to protect it. Child profile and learning information stays on the device. Parents should keep the device up to date and use its screen lock to protect saved information.

## 10. Children and changes to this policy

Children can use the learning activities without giving us personal information or creating an online account. The app may use an optional nickname on the device, but we do not knowingly collect a child’s personal information online through the current app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will review the request and delete it where appropriate.

If a future feature needs to collect or share a child’s personal information, we will update this policy and obtain parental permission where applicable before enabling that collection. Privacy laws differ by country and may give families additional rights. We will update the date above when this policy changes and provide an in-app notice for important changes.

## Publisher review before public release

The app publisher must add its legal name, postal address, and a telephone contact, configure an approved Google consent message and Ad Review Center controls, and complete legal and store disclosure review before public release. The publisher remains responsible for Google Play Data safety and App Store privacy disclosures and must verify Google’s current data handling and child-directed practices in each launch market. This draft describes the current implementation but is not legal advice.
