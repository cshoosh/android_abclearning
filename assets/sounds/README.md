# Animal sound credits

## Original interface effects

`ui-pop.mp3` (soft bubble-pop marimba sparkle) and `ui-success.mp3` (two-note win chime) were generated for ABC Friends with Stable Audio 3.0 on October 1, 2026. Both are original, short instrumental effects with no supplied samples; WAV downloads were encoded as 96 kbps MP3s. The app plays them for shared button taps, balloon/letter-bubble pops and earned activity rewards. Parents can turn interface effects off in Settings.

Generation prompts:

- **Pop:** “A single soft, rounded cartoon bubble pop sound effect with a tiny warm wooden marimba sparkle after it. Friendly and gentle for a preschool learning game button tap. One quick clean sound, under one second, quiet, no voice, no music, no background, original sound.”
- **Success:** “A tiny cheerful success sound effect for a preschool learning game: two soft ascending marimba notes with a delicate bell sparkle, warm and encouraging, gentle volume, short and clean under one second. Instrumental sound only, no speech, no music bed, original.”

Stable Audio output terms, commercial registration and attribution requirements are covered in [the music notes](../music/README.md) and [third-party notices](../../docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Added alphabet-animal cues

These sounds fill the gaps in the A–Z animal set. The four Stable Audio clips are original, short AI-generated learning cues, not field recordings or verified biological calls. In particular, Newts and X-ray tetras should not be presented as making the cue sounds in nature, and Unicorn is a fictional animal. Stable Audio generated the source WAVs on October 1, 2026; each was converted to mono 96 kbps MP3 and faded out briefly.

| App asset | Description | Source and license |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `iguana.mp3` | Gentle hiss and breathy cough cue | Original Stable Audio 3.0 generation; see prompt below |
| `newt.mp3` | Quiet clicks followed by a small bubble cue | Original Stable Audio 3.0 generation; see prompt below |
| `unicorn.mp3` | Soft horse-like whicker, hoof shuffle and bell shimmer | Original Stable Audio 3.0 generation; see prompt below |
| `x-ray-fish.mp3` | Delicate underwater tick and small bubbles | Original Stable Audio 3.0 generation; see prompt below |
| `yak.mp3` | Short yak-grunt imitation | AntumDeluge, [Yak Imitation](https://opengameart.org/content/yak-imitation), CC0; source file transcoded from OGG to MP3 |

Generation prompts:

- **Iguana:** “Short naturalistic iguana warning sound: a soft close hiss followed by one little breathy cough, dry and quiet, no roar, no reptile from a film or game, no music, no other animal, original isolated animal sound.”
- **Newt:** “A tiny soft underwater sound cue for a newt: two very quiet dry ticks/clicks followed by a little water bubble, subtle and gentle. Not a frog croak, no bird, no music or voices, brief original nature effect.”
- **Unicorn:** “A warm, gentle storybook unicorn cue: one soft horse-like whicker and hoof shuffle followed by a small twinkly bell shimmer. Sweet, brief and child-friendly, no music, no voice, no reference to any existing character or franchise, original isolated effect.”
- **X-ray fish:** “A tiny aquarium X-ray tetra cue: one delicate underwater tick and a couple of tiny soft bubbles, light and curious, no vocal animal call, no music, no voice, no sci-fi, original.”

Stable Audio's hosted-service tier restrictions for generated clips are recorded in [the music notes](../music/README.md) and [third-party notices](../../docs/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
