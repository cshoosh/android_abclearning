ABC Friends Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

ABC Friends is an offline-first learning app for children and caregivers. Children can use learning activities without creating an account. A child's optional nickname, avatar, settings and learning progress stay on the device. On Android, a non-personalized ad may appear only in the parent-gated dashboard while Premium is inactive. An adult can make or restore a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play. Existing subscribers may keep access through their paid period.

Who this policy is for

This policy explains what information stays on the device, what service providers may process, and how parents can manage or delete information saved by ABC Friends.

App: ABC Friends
Privacy contact: info@021labs.online

Information kept on the device

A parent may choose a short nickname and an animal avatar for a child. ABC Friends also saves the selected language and sound settings, letters viewed or traced, activities played, stars and stickers. This information stays on the device. ABC Friends does not send a child's nickname, avatar or learning progress to us.

Please avoid nicknames that include a child's full name, school, address, phone number or other private details. The operating system or a device backup service may copy app data according to the family's device settings; that backup is controlled by the device provider.

Speech and sound

When voice is turned on, ABC Friends asks the device's built-in text-to-speech service to speak letters and words. The app does not record a child's voice or use speech recognition. The device or speech-service provider may handle the text under its own settings and privacy terms. Bundled animal sounds play on the device and are not uploaded by ABC Friends.

What ABC Friends does not use

ABC Friends does not run its own behavioral advertising or user analytics, social features, public child profiles or child-to-child messaging. It does not ask for a child's precise location, contacts, photos, camera access or microphone recordings. We do not sell children's information.

Service providers and sharing

We do not share a child's profile or learning progress. On Android, Google Mobile Ads and its consent tools may automatically collect and share approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions such as launches and taps, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Ads are child-directed, non-personalized and limited to the parent area. The Android advertising-ID permission is blocked, but other identifiers may still be processed. Google Play and RevenueCat process adult purchase history and purchase status, including any legacy subscription still in its paid period; they do not receive a child's nickname, profile or learning progress.

If a parent emails us, we receive the email address and message so we can reply. We may disclose information if required by law or when necessary to protect a person's safety, the app or our legal rights. Apple, Google and device operating-system providers may separately process information when families use their services; their privacy policies apply.

Purchases and ads

An adult can make a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play. Google Play shows the localized price before purchase and charges the Google Play account once; this purchase does not renew. Existing subscribers may retain Premium through the end of a period they have already paid for; Google Play manages any such legacy subscription renewal or cancellation. Google Play handles payment details; RevenueCat verifies purchase history to unlock Premium and restore access. ABC Friends does not collect card details.

A non-personalized Google Mobile Ads banner may appear only in the parent-gated dashboard on Android while Premium is inactive. It does not appear in child activities and is not rewarded. Google Play, RevenueCat, Google Mobile Ads and Google consent tools process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

How long information is kept and deletion

Information stored on the device remains there until a parent resets the relevant app data, clears ABC Friends from the device or uninstalls the app. Reset all progress clears learning activity, stars and stickers; the child's nickname, avatar and preferences remain. To remove all app data, use the device's app-storage controls or uninstall ABC Friends. A device backup may retain a copy according to device-provider settings.

We keep support emails only as long as needed to answer and follow up on a request or meet legal and security duties.

Parents' choices and rights

Parents can turn off voice in app settings and reset the child's local learning progress at any time. Rights available under local privacy law may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a complaint to a privacy regulator.

Security

We limit the information the app needs and use reasonable safeguards. Child profile and learning information stays on the device. Parents should keep the device up to date and use its screen lock to protect saved information.

Children and policy changes

Children can use learning activities without giving us personal information or creating an online account. The app may use an optional nickname on the device, but we do not knowingly collect a child's personal information online through the current app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will review the request and delete it where appropriate.

If a future feature needs to collect or share a child's personal information, we will update this policy and obtain parental permission where applicable before enabling that collection. Privacy laws differ by country and may give families additional rights. We will update the date above when this policy changes and provide an in-app notice for important changes.

Contact

A parent or guardian can contact us about a privacy question or concern at info@021labs.online.
